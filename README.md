The one line a smart fridge would text its owner at 3am.

## The entry

> 3am. I hummed all night so you could sleep, and you have opened me four times and eaten nothing. We need to talk.

113 characters, one line, plain ASCII — inside the 1–140 character gate.

## Why this line

Written against the judge's brief, in the order it weighs things:

1. **A one-line text a smart fridge sends its owner at 3am, and funny** — it opens with "3am." and reads complete on a lock screen, no follow-up needed.
2. **The bigger laugh** — an appliance staging a relationship talk at 3am is the line a reader screenshots and sends to a friend.
3. **Sounds like a fridge** — a small voice with an opinion and no shame about the hour: it kept its side of the bargain, humming all night, and it has a grievance.
4. **Works as one line of a text message** — short, self-contained, and the punchline ("We need to talk.") closes it.
5. **Fresh angle** — not milk, not ice, not a door left open: the fridge as the neglected partner in the house, finally deciding that 3am is the moment to speak.
